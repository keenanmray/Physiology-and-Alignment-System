Becoming

Become more aligned, energized, and intentional each day.

Becoming helps you connect who you want to be with how you actually live. Set your intention, log your physiology, and get a clear score with grounded next steps.

What it does

It turns daily intention and physiology into a simple readout and 3 actionable steps that help you move toward the person you want to become.

Morning Setup

Begin with identity and direction, then log the physical signals that shape your energy, performance, and consistency.

Quick Guide

If you are new to this, use these cues. You do not need perfect answers.

North Star

A simple direction for your life or this season. Example: “Build a healthier, calmer, stronger life.”

Why It Matters

The reason underneath the goal. Example: “I want more peace, energy, and self-respect.”

Stress 0-5

`0` = very calm, `3` = moderate stress, `5` = overwhelmed.

Training 0-5

`0` = rest day, `2` = light/moderate, `5` = very intense.

Social Quality 1-5

`1` = draining/disconnected, `3` = okay, `5` = energizing and meaningful.

Location

If you do not know latitude/longitude, keep the default or use your city’s coordinates from Maps or Google.

Morning Intention

Set direction before the day speeds up.

Name the purpose behind the day and define three tiny steps that move your life forward. If you do not know your long-term North Star yet, just name what matters in this season of life.

Behavior Inputs

Log the signals that shape recovery and readiness.

Use rough, honest estimates. This works best when it is simple enough to do consistently, not perfectly.

Your Daily Readout

One clear score, a simple interpretation, and the next actions that matter most today.
