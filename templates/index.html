Becoming Beta

Become more aligned, energized, and intentional each day.

Becoming helps you connect who you want to be with how you actually live. Set your intention, log your physiology, and get a clear score with grounded next steps.

What it does

It turns daily intention and physiology into a simple readout and 3 actionable steps that help you move toward the person you want to become.

Morning Setup

Begin with identity and direction, then log the physical signals that shape your energy, performance, and consistency.

Morning Intention

Set direction before the day speeds up.

Name the purpose behind the day and define three tiny steps that move your life forward.

Behavior Inputs

Log the signals that shape recovery and readiness.

Use normal clock times for sleep and timing-based behaviors so the app feels more like real life.
