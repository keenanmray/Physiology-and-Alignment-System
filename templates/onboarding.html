You become what you repeatedly believe you are.

Most apps track what you do. Becoming starts with who you are becoming — then grounds that in your actual biology. The result is a daily readout that speaks to both at once.

Research in psychology shows that our self-concept — how we genuinely think and feel about ourselves — determines what actions feel natural to us. A strong, clear identity generates strong, aligned behavior automatically.

Becoming is built on this. Every day you clarify who you're becoming, log the biology that shapes your energy, and Claude bridges both into three grounded actions. Do this daily and the compounding is real.

Who are you becoming? Not your goals — your identity. The person you are actively building toward. This is the most important thing you'll write.

Before you start — think about your North Star.

Don't rush this. Your North Star isn't a goal — it's an identity statement. Who is the person you are actively choosing to become?

"I am a healthy, grounded, and formidable person. I show up fully in all areas of my life and build things that matter."

Write it in the present tense, as if it's already true. That's not delusion — that's how identity change actually works.
